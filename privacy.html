Privacy Policy

Hermes Google Workspace Assistant · Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Hermes Google Workspace Assistant is a personal, self-hosted integration operated by the account owner on a private computer. It is not a public multi-user service.

Google data requested

Depending on the enabled Hermes features and the permissions granted by the account owner, the integration may request access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Sheets, and Google Docs. The current OAuth configuration determines the exact scopes shown on Google's consent screen.

How Google data is used

Google data is used only to complete tasks explicitly requested by the account owner, such as reading or creating calendar events, searching authorized files, or preparing documents. It is not used for advertising, sale, credit decisions, or generalized profiling.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials and local working data are stored on the owner's access-controlled computer. Relevant content may be sent to the AI model provider selected by the owner when needed to generate a response. No Google data is sold or shared with unrelated third parties.

The owner is responsible for securing the computer, reviewing agent actions, and choosing the AI provider used by the local installation.

Retention and deletion

Local session, log, and working files may remain on the owner's computer according to the owner's local configuration. The owner may delete local files and revoke Google access at any time. Revocation is available from Google Account permissions.

Contact

This personal installation does not operate a public support service. Project information is available in the source repository.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
