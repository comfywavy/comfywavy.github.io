Hermes
Google Workspace Assistant

A private, owner-operated connection between Hermes Agent and Google Workspace. It performs only the Google tasks requested by the account owner.

What this app does

Hermes runs on the owner's own computer and uses Google APIs to carry out explicitly requested productivity tasks, such as managing calendar events and working with authorized Workspace data.

  • Access is granted directly by the account owner through Google OAuth.
  • The app is not a public service and has no public sign-up.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
  • No advertising, data sale, or generalized profile-building is performed.
